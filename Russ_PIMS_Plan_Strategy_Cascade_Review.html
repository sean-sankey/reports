How the PIMS plan stands up as a strategy

Russ Bailey's draft plan (the Word version, 11 sections) and the PIMS brochure, read against the five-part strategy cascade from our first session: ambition, where to play, how to win, capabilities and management systems.

AS AN OPERATIONAL PLAN
A solid, operational-style growth plan

3 headlines

Clear, classic pillars. Protect, Grow, Cross-sell, Productise and Profit give the plan a familiar, credible spine.

Numbers that hang together. An honest baseline, a Year 1 income bridge that adds up, and margins that build year on year.

Strong control mechanisms. Guardrails on recruitment, pricing and debt show a board that growth won't run ahead of income.

AS A STRATEGY
A good plan, a thin strategy
Average of the five cascade scores

3 headlines

Strong on control and delivery. The financial guardrails and operating detail are credible and close to board-ready. This plays to Russ's strengths as an Implementer.

Thin where strategy is hardest. The plan never says why a client picks PIMS over the alternatives, and it never says what PIMS won't do. 40 listed items, no stated choices not to play.

The fix is choosing, not more writing. The five cascade boxes on one page at the front, in Russ's own words, then one consistent shape all the way through the Word document.

WHAT TO DO NEXT

🪜 Five moves to lift it

Practical steps, in order. Each one builds on the last.

1

One page at the front

The five cascade boxes, 25 words each, in Russ's words. The first page the board sees.
2

A "we will / we won't" per box

One of each. The "won't" lines are where the strategy shows.
3

Write How to win first

Three "we win because" lines, lifted from the brochure, each with a client example. Test them on two client calls.
4

One shape, all the way through

Use the cascade as the structure of the Word document, so every section hangs off the same five boxes.
5

Keep the Word version

Drop the slide version. Number every list, add confidence ratings to the targets, and cut the board scorecard to five measures.

📄 The front page

Headers only, ready to fill, each with a prompt to get started.

A prompt for AI, if it helps: "Here is my plan. Using only what's in it, draft one sentence for each of these five headings. Then list every place the plan implies a choice we won't make. Don't add anything new." Then rewrite every line in your own words.
